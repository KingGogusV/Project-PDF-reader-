# Backlog

## Critical
- Verify original-byte preservation and supported annotation/form export-reopen before claiming edits work.
- Enforce permission, scripting and signed-document boundaries.
## High Priority
- Complete local opening, accurate rendering, navigation, text selection, search and error recovery.
- Verify adaptive reader controls across desktop, tablet and phone dimensions.
## Medium Priority
- Verify multi-document state, offline shell, print/export and recent-file behavior.
## Low Priority
- Additional themes and optional reader preferences after usability validation.
## Platform Gaps
- Physical Safari/iOS/iPadOS and Android testing; macOS/Linux verification.
- Native packaging, file associations, filesystem atomic writes and native sharing.
## Research
- Extended document compatibility corpus, assistive reading order, performance on constrained hardware.
## Deferred
- OCR, page organization, merge/split, compression, true redaction, PDF/A, content editing, certificate signatures and collaboration.
- Underline/strikethrough and other editor types not safely supported by the chosen engine pipeline.

This is a roadmap, not blanket authorization for unlimited feature expansion.
